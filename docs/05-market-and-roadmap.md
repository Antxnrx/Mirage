# 05 · Users, market and roadmap

## Users

| Segment | Need |
|---|---|
| **ZEC holders who use exchanges** | Cash out without linking their shielded activity to their identity |
| **Zcash wallet teams** | A drop-in pre-send check, so a wallet doesn't ship a linkable flow by accident |
| **Swap and bridge services** (e.g. NEAR Intents routes) | Deposit and refund flows that don't undo the user's privacy |
| **High-risk users** (journalists, activists, NGOs) | Being linked can be dangerous, not just inconvenient |

> Replace with real conversations from the venue before submission. We don't list users we haven't spoken to.

## Sustainability
- **Open source:** `mirage-core` and the web app are open source.
- **Grant funding:** Zcash Community Grants, the coinholder-directed retroactive grants, and ecosystem partners.
- **Paid work:** integration and maintenance work for wallets; a hosted index for swap services.
- **Beyond Zcash:** the same boundary analysis applies to other shielded-pool systems.
